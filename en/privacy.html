Privacy policy

In short

Open Remote PRO does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and sends no data to the developer.

Data kept on your device

  • Your list of computers (name, address, port, user) and settings: stored only on the device.
  • Passwords and SSH keys: stored only on the device, in the Keychain (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or encrypted with Android Keystore (Android), and excluded from cloud backups.
  • Fingerprints of accepted host keys and certificates: on the device.

Network connections

The app connects only to the computers you add or pick from those found on your local network. Screen images, keystrokes, pointer movements, clipboard contents and management commands travel directly between your device and those computers, never through the developer's servers.

Security in transit: SSH and RDP are encrypted; high-performance video is encrypted with SRTP; on the standard VNC/RFB channel, data after authentication may travel unencrypted. Use a VPN on untrusted networks.

Permissions

  • Local network (iPhone, iPad, Mac): to find computers with screen sharing turned on and connect to them.
  • Add to Photo Library (optional): only to save a snapshot of the remote screen when you ask for it.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (16 in the European Union) and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights (GDPR)

The developer holds no personal data about users. For any question, write to deduzzo@gmail.com.
